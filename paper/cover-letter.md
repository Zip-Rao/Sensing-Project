Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Short-pulse sequences as local probes for
superconducting-qubit frequency calibration," for publication in Physical
Review A.

The manuscript investigates whether short-pulse sequences can contribute
directly to qubit calibration through the parameter response accumulated during
the pulses.  A two-branch, zero-delay sequence provides a concrete local
frequency discriminator.  A full third-order response kernel calibrates its
nonlinear inverse, while drive-reference tracking keeps the probe within its
valid window during feedback.  Numerical benchmarks identify a regime in which
the short-pulse protocol reaches a common calibration tolerance with 4.1 times
fewer instrumented solver calls than a double-sweep Ramsey baseline, while also
showing why a simple coarse-to-fine handoff is insufficient.

The present letter is a working draft.  Originality, author approval, related
manuscripts, funding, data availability, and corresponding-author information
