# PRA working checklist

- Confirm final scientific scope and title.
- Confirm authorship, author order, corresponding author, affiliations, and ORCID.
- Verify all bibliography entries against publisher or DOI metadata.
- Decide whether the two-level model is sufficient or add a leakage comparison.
- Add shot noise, readout error, drift, pulse distortion, and parameter uncertainty.
- Report common-threshold cost and do not rely on nominal workflow accounting.
- Replace software-specific names in figures where a physics description is clearer.
- Regenerate figures at final column-aware dimensions with consistent typography.
- Add data/code availability and exact funding language.
- Compile with no undefined references, citations, or overfull boxes.
