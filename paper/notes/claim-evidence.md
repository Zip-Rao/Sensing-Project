## Important wording boundary

Use "solver-cost reduction in numerical simulations" for the current result.
Do not write "experimental speedup" until real acquisition time and uncertainty
are available.  Describe the broader short-pulse claim as a demonstrated
possibility in one frequency-calibration case, not as a universal calibration
replacement.
